[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- Introduction -[/b][/size][/color]

RadioXL lets you add your own radio stations to Cyberpunk 2077, and they behave like the game's own. Each one sits on the dial at its own frequency and plays in cars, on the Radioport and on the radios out in the world. It runs on the game's own radio, so the station popup, the dashboard, the radio keys and every moment the game mutes the radio all work the same as they do for a vanilla station. Tune away and come back later, and the station is wherever it would have got to.

A station is one station.json and a folder of songs. No yaml, no scripts, no sound banks.

You also get an equalizer for the radio, and the Library, where you pick exactly which song plays on any station. Then there are shards: playlists you make yourself from any song you have installed, the game's included.

[i]TL;DR - your music, on every radio in the game, through the game's own radio. (ﾉ◕ヮ◕)ﾉ[/i]


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- For Players -[/b][/size][/color]

RadioXL is a framework. On its own it gives you the equalizer, shards and its settings, but no new stations. Install it, then add stations built for it, or make your own with the station builder further down. It takes a few minutes, icon included.

[b]Install[/b]
Install it with your mod manager. Keep the plugin and the scripts together: if one is missing, the scripts fail to compile at launch.

[b]Uninstall[/b]
Remove RadioXL and every station built on it. RadioXL writes nothing of its own into your saves. A radio in the world that was left on a custom station keeps that station's place in the list, so it can come back on a different station.

The source is on GitHub: [url=https://github.com/spuddeh/cp2077-radio-xl]github.com/spuddeh/cp2077-radio-xl[/url]. The full station.json reference is [url=https://github.com/spuddeh/cp2077-radio-xl/blob/main/red4ext/plugins/RadioXL/stations/README.md]stations/README.md[/url].


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- The radio -[/b][/size][/color]

Open the radio, in a car or on foot, and it has three tabs: [b]Radioport[/b] (the game's station list), [b]Library[/b] and [b]Equalizer[/b]. LB and RB, 1 and 3 on a keyboard, or a click on a tab switch between them. The station list works with the mouse too: the wheel scrolls it, pointing at a station selects it and a click plays it.


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- The equalizer -[/b][/size][/color]

Nine bands from 63 Hz to 16 kHz, on the car radio, the Radioport and the metro. It starts on Flat, which is the game's own sound.

[list]
[*][b]Presets.[/b] Sixteen to start with: Flat, Bass boost, Treble boost, Vocal, Loudness, and one for each kind of music the game's stations play. Or move the bands yourself.[/*]
[*][b]Per-station EQ.[/b] Turn it on and every station keeps its own equalizer: the one you set for it, or the preset suggested for that station. Growl FM, a mix of everything, plays Flat.[/*]
[*][b]Processing.[/b] What a radio station does to its sound before it goes out: [b]Even out loudness[/b], [b]Soften peaks[/b] and a [b]Limiter[/b], each with its own switch. All three together is a station's full broadcast processing.[/*]
[*][b]Volume boost.[/b] Up to 12 dB louder than the game's own maximum, for the car radio and the Radioport.[/*]
[*][b]Your own presets.[/b] A preset is a small file with a name and nine numbers. Copy one, change it, and it shows up in the list next time you start the game. Share them like any other file.[/*]
[*][b]Reset.[/b] Puts the station back on its suggested preset, or the equalizer back on Flat.[/*]
[*][b]Mouse, keyboard or controller.[/b] Click the controls, or use the arrow keys or d-pad to move between them and Enter or A to press one.[/*]
[/list]


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- Shards -[/b][/size][/color]

A shard is a playlist of your own, made from any song you have installed, the game's included. It plays on a station called Shard, last on the dial, on the Radioport, in vehicles and on radios in the world.

[i]Internally it goes by Spuddehfy, because a few of you wouldn't stop calling it that.[/i]

[b]The Library[/b]
The Library tab is where you pick exactly what plays. It shows your shards, and Tab swaps it to every station instead. Pick a shard or a station, then pick any song on it and press E or click it: that song plays from the start, straight away. After it, the station carries on picking at random as usual, and a shard set to play in order carries on in order. All songs holds every song you have once, and the filter box narrows the list as you type.

[b]The shard builder[/b]
Press R on a shard in the Library, or pick + NEW, to open the shard builder.
[list]
[*]Your shards are tabs along the top. LB and RB, or a click, change shard. + NEW makes a new one.[/*]
[*]Three columns: where songs come from (All songs and every station), that source's songs, and the shard in play order. A and D, the d-pad or the mouse move between them.[/*]
[*]E or a click plays a song. R, X on a controller, or the box at the end of a row adds a song to the shard or takes it off.[/*]
[*]The arrows on a row, or LT and RT on a controller, move a song up or down the shard.[/*]
[*]Play order switches the shard between in order and shuffle.[/*]
[*]Each shard keeps its own equalizer, the same as a station does with Per-station EQ on.[/*]
[*]Click the shard's name, or use the rename key at the bottom of the screen, to rename it. Renaming needs a keyboard.[/*]
[*]The delete key at the bottom of the screen deletes the shard after you confirm. Its songs stay on their stations and in your other shards.[/*]
[*]Back returns to the Library. Every change saves as you make it.[/*]
[/list]
A shard you empty of songs is deleted when you leave it. Stations that stream aren't offered as sources.


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- The station builder -[/b][/size][/color]

[url=https://spuddeh.github.io/cp2077-radio-xl/]The station builder[/url] is a web page that makes a station mod for you. Nothing to install: it runs in your browser and gives you a zip ready for your mod manager.

[list]
[*][b]A new station.[/b] Give it a name and a frequency, drop in your songs, mark any of them as idents, and download the zip. The page shows the station on a Radioport and on a world radio as you go, so you can see the label and the icon before the game does.[/*]
[*][b]Levelled for you.[/b] Auto level measures every song and sets its level to match the game's own stations, a loud one down and a quiet one up, without touching the files. A play button lets you hear each track at the level set. A file already mastered to full scale can't be raised, so give the station a listen and adjust anything that sounds off.[/*]
[*][b]WAVs fixed for you.[/b] A WAV that won't play in game, such as a 24-bit or 32-bit float file, is flagged as soon as you add it, and the page converts it to 16-bit, one at a time or all at once. Build .zip waits until every one is converted or removed.[/*]
[*][b]An icon from a picture.[/b] Choose an image and the page makes the icon for you. Or pick one of the game's own station icons, or keep the RadioXL glyph.[/*]
[*][b]A stream station.[/b] Paste the stream URL and play it to check it's the right one. The page gives you the exact lines to add to AudioXL's settings, every host included.[/*]
[*][b]From RadioExt.[/b] Drop a RadioExt station's zip, rar or 7z on the page and it comes across as a RadioXL station: name, frequency, songs and icon.[/*]
[*][b]Open a station you made.[/b] Drop its zip back on the page to change it.[/*]
[*][b]Songs you add later.[/b] "Add songs I drop into this folder later" makes RadioXL pick up audio files you put in the station's folder at each launch, and drop listed ones that are gone.[/*]
[/list]
The page checks everything RadioXL would refuse before it lets you build, and tells you what to fix.


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- Adding a station -[/b][/size][/color]

A station is one folder under
[code]red4ext\plugins\RadioXL\stations\<YourMod>\[/code]
holding a [b]station.json[/b] and the audio files it names.

[b]Start from the template.[/b] [i]RadioXL Example Station[/i] in the optional downloads is a working station: install it and "102.7 Example Radio" is on the dial. Change its station.json and swap the audio, and it's yours.

[quote][code]{
  "name": "radio_station_example",
  "frequency": 102.7,
  "displayName": "Example Radio",
  "tracks": [
    { "file": "audio/track_01.mp3", "title": "Example Radio - First Song" },
    { "file": "audio/track_02.mp3", "title": "Example Radio - Second Song" },
    { "file": "audio/ident.mp3", "ident": true }
  ]
}[/code]
[/quote]

[list]
[*][b]name[/b] (Required). The station's ID: letters, digits and underscores, different from every other installed station.[/*]
[*][b]frequency[/b] (Required). Where the station sits on the dial. Pick one the game doesn't use: 88.9, 89.3, 89.7, 91.9, 92.9, 95.2, 96.1, 98.7, 99.9, 101.9, 102.0, 103.5, 106.9, 107.3 and 107.5 are taken.[/*]
[*][b]displayName[/b] (Required). The station's name, without the frequency. The game shows them together: [b]104.9 Your Station[/b].[/*]
[*][b]tracks[/b] (Required). The songs, played in random order. MP3, OGG, FLAC, or WAV as 16-bit PCM. Each track is a [b]file[/b], or a [b]url[/b] for a live MP3 stream as the station's only track.[/*]
[*][b]title[/b] (Per track). The song name in the radio popup.[/*]
[*][b]ident[/b] (Per track). true marks a station ident, jingle or ad. One plays after every third song.[/*]
[*][b]icon[/b], [b]atlas[/b] (Optional). The station's icon. The builder makes both from a picture. Leave them out and the station shows the RadioXL glyph.[/*]
[*][b]gain[/b] (Optional). A level, on the station or on a track: 1 as recorded, up to 4. The builder sets it for you.[/*]
[*][b]news[/b] (Optional). true lets Stanley's bulletins and N54 News reach the station.[/*]
[*][b]addUnlistedFiles[/b] (Optional). true adds audio files you drop in the station's folder at each launch.[/*]
[/list]
[b]Audio formats.[/b] MP3, OGG, FLAC and WAV. A WAV has to be 16-bit PCM: a 24-bit or 32-bit float WAV, the default export in some editors, plays silence, so RadioXL leaves it out of the station and names it in its log. The station builder converts one to 16-bit for you.

A few more fields are in [url=https://github.com/spuddeh/cp2077-radio-xl/blob/main/red4ext/plugins/RadioXL/stations/README.md]stations/README.md[/url]. A station.json with a mistake in it is skipped, and the log names the file and the line.


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- Stations that stream -[/b][/size][/color]

A station can play a live internet radio stream instead of music files. AudioXL doesn't go online until you allow it, in [b]red4ext\plugins\AudioXL\AudioXL.ini[/b]:
[quote][code][Network]
allowHttpConnections = true
allowedHost = ice1.somafm.com[/code]
[/quote]
Add one [b]allowedHost[/b] line per host. A [b]*.somafm.com[/b] line covers every host under that domain, but not [b]somafm.com[/b] itself, which needs its own line.

[b]A stream can redirect to another host.[/b] If a stream station stays silent with its host allowed, check AudioXL's log, [b]red4ext\logs\audioxl-<date>.log[/b], for the host it refused:
[quote][code][AudioXL] Register 'radio_station_example_01' (script): 'regiocast.streamabc.net' is not in the allowedHost list in AudioXL.ini[/code]
[/quote]
Add that host and restart the game. AudioXL only reads the file at startup, and it makes a new log every launch.

AudioXL creates AudioXL.ini the first time it starts, with everything off. Under Mod Organizer 2 the file is in Overwrite.

A stream station joins the broadcast live when you tune in, after a second or two of buffering, and shows the station's name rather than the song playing.

When a stream station can't play, RadioXL shows a red warning in game a few seconds after loading, telling you what to change.


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- For mod authors -[/b][/size][/color]

Other mods can read RadioXL's stations and songs, change what plays and listen for changes, from redscript or CET. The documentation, with examples for both: [url=https://github.com/spuddeh/cp2077-radio-xl/blob/main/docs/script-api.md]docs/script-api.md[/url].


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- Settings -[/b][/size][/color]

Five tabs in the Redscript Configuration Framework panel. Press a key or a controller button to bind one, and Escape clears it. Pick a key the game doesn't already use.

[b]Controls[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Next song / Previous song.[/b] In a vehicle they work on the vehicle radio, the metro's included, and on foot on the Radioport. Default: F3 / F2.[/*]
[*][b]Next station / Previous station.[/b] Move along the dial in the radio's order, skipping stations set aside on the Stations tab. Default: Unbound.[/*]
[*][b]Show what's playing.[/b] Puts the radio popup on screen for the song playing now. Default: Unbound.[/*]
[*][b]Never play this song again.[/b] Switches the song playing off and moves on. Default: Unbound.[/*]
[*][b]Jump to my station.[/b] Tunes straight to the station on the My station tab. Default: Unbound.[/*]
[*][b]Hold a modifier with the keys.[/b] Every key gets a second row for a key held with it, so F3 can be the next song and Shift+F3 the next station. Default: Off.[/*]
[*][b]Separate keys for the Radioport.[/b] Gives the Radioport its own set of keys, used on foot. Default: Off.[/*]
[*][b]Show the song on the Radioport.[/b] The song popup shows for the Radioport too, when a song changes. Default: Off.[/*]
[*][b]Show the song as an on-screen message.[/b] The station and song at the left of the screen when a song changes. Default: Off.[/*]
[/list]

[b]My station[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Tune to my station.[/b] Turns this tab on. Default: Off.[/*]
[*][b]Station.[/b] The station to tune to. It's remembered by name, so it stays the same station when you add or remove station mods. Default: None.[/*]
[*][b]When getting into a car.[/b] Tunes when you get in the driver's seat, and switches the car radio on if it's off. Default: On.[/*]
[*][b]When the car radio is switched on.[/b] Tunes when you turn the car radio on. Default: On.[/*]
[*][b]When the Radioport is switched on.[/b] Tunes when you turn the Radioport on. Default: On.[/*]
[*][b]Even if the Radioport is on.[/b] Tunes the car radio even when the Radioport is playing another station. Default: On.[/*]
[/list]
Nothing is tuned while the game blocks the radio: in a police vehicle, during a scripted drive, as Johnny, and before the car radio is unlocked.

[b]Stations[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Custom stations on traffic cars.[/b] Which passing cars can be tuned to a custom station: Most cars, Every car or Off. Default: Most cars.[/*]
[*][b]World radios can start on a custom station.[/b] Radios and jukeboxes that start on a random station can land on a custom one. Default: On.[/*]
[*][b]Include stations that stream.[/b] Lets traffic cars and world radios pick a station that streams. Default: Off.[/*]
[*][b]Impulse's DJ set in All songs.[/b] Adds Impulse's single long DJ set to the All songs shard. Default: Off.[/*]
[*][b]Keys step over this station.[/b] One per station: the next and previous station keys skip it. Default: Off.[/*]
[*][b]Each song.[/b] One row per song, the game's included: On, Off (skipped whenever it comes up) or Off while streaming (skipped while the game's Streamer Mode is on). Default: On, or Off while streaming for songs the game marks as not streamer friendly.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Sound[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Volume boost (dB).[/b] Turns the car radio and the Radioport up by as much as 12 dB past the game's own maximum. Default: 0.[/*]
[*][b]Even out loudness.[/b] Holds songs at a similar loudness. While someone speaks and Someone is speaking is on, it lets the game's dip through. Default: Off.[/*]
[*][b]Soften peaks.[/b] Takes the edge off sudden hits such as drums. Default: Off.[/*]
[*][b]Limiter.[/b] A ceiling for the radio's loudest moments. Default: Off.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Mute[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Mute station idents.[/b] The station's own spot between songs: its name, its frequency and whatever its host says over them. Default: Off.[/*]
[*][b]Mute DJ announcements.[/b] Stanley's news, Maximum Mike's song intros and Growl FM's Ash. Default: Off.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Mute the radio when...[/b] Thirteen switches, all on by default, which is what the game does. Turn one off and the radio keeps playing through that situation, on every station.
[list]
[*]A call is in progress: Radioport.[/*]
[*]A scene is playing: Radioport.[/*]
[*]A driving scene is playing: Radioport.[/*]
[*]You are inside a club: Radioport.[/*]
[*]You are in a weapons-free area: Radioport.[/*]
[*]A quest has blocked the radio: Radioport.[/*]
[*]Megabuilding H10's music is playing: Radioport.[/*]
[*]Combat music is playing: Car and Radioport.[/*]
[*]Police music is playing: Car and Radioport.[/*]
[*]Someone is speaking: Car and Radioport.[/*]
[*]A gameplay menu is open: Car and Radioport.[/*]
[*]The pause menu is open: Car and Radioport.[/*]
[*]A wheel or popup is open: Car and Radioport.[/*]
[/list]


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- Troubleshooting -[/b][/size][/color]

Every launch writes a new set of logs, with the date and time in the name, so open the newest one from the launch that had the problem. Under Mod Organizer 2 they're in the Overwrite folder, not the game folder.

[list]
[*][b]red4ext\logs\radioxl-<date>.log[/b] - RadioXL's main log. Every station it found and how many songs it has, every mistake in a station.json, and whether it matched your game version: a working install says [i]every byte check passed[/i].[/*]
[*][b]red4ext\logs\audioxl-<date>.log[/b] - AudioXL's log. A refused stream host shows up here.[/*]
[*][b]r6\logs\mods\RadioXL__<date>.log[/b] and [b]r6\logs\mods\AudioXL__<date>.log[/b] - Only with RedLogger installed. What each mod registered as the game loaded.[/*]
[*][b]r6\logs\redscript_rCURRENT.log[/b] - Script errors. If the game shows a script error at launch, the reason is in here, usually a missing requirement, or RadioXL's plugin and scripts not installed together.[/*]
[/list]

[list]
[*][b]A station is not showing up.[/b] Open radioxl-<date>.log. A mistake shows as [i]YourMod/station.json:<line>: what is wrong[/i], and that station is skipped until you fix it. No line for the station at all means RadioXL didn't find it: check it's in red4ext\plugins\RadioXL\stations\<YourMod>\.[/*]
[*][b]A station sorts oddly next to another.[/b] The two share a frequency. The log says which two.[/*]
[*][b]A stream station is silent.[/b] Its host has to be allowed in AudioXL.ini (see Stations that stream). audioxl-<date>.log names any host it refused.[/*]
[*][b]A key does nothing.[/b] Keys don't work in menus or photo mode, and the song and station keys only work with a radio on. Check the key is bound on the Controls tab, not only in the game's own settings, and that its modifier is held if you use one. If the Stations tab says the station list hasn't been read, load a save and open the panel again.[/*]
[*][b]A song cuts off early or is followed by silence.[/b] RadioXL remembers each song's length in red4ext\plugins\RadioXL\cache.json (under MO2, in Overwrite). A file swapped for another with the same size and date keeps the old length. Delete cache.json and launch again. It's always safe to delete.[/*]
[*][b]A radio in the world is silent.[/b] Radios placed in the world follow the game's sound effects volume, not the music volume. With sound effects turned down they go quiet while the Radioport still plays. The game's own stations do the same.[/*]
[/list]


[color=#e06666][size=6][b]- Known Limits -[/b][/size][/color]

[list]
[*]News bulletins go to one news station at a time, picked by the game from every news station with a radio nearby. In the city a nearby game station often gets it, so news is only dependable away from other radios.[/*]
[*]Up to 101 custom stations.[/*]
[*]A radio placed in the world, your apartment's included, saves its station by its place in the station list. Add or remove a station mod, or update RadioXL, and it can come back on a different station: just tune it again. Car radios and the Radioport remember a station by name.[/*]
[*]A passing traffic car's radio is tuned to a station but you don't hear it in the game.[/*]
[*]RadioXL replaces the game's station cycling, so it can't be installed alongside RadioExt or RadioXL 0.1.0.[/*]
[/list]
